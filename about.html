About Broadway UMC

A faithful UMC presence in Broadway, North Carolina.

Who We Are

Broadway United Methodist Church is a welcoming congregation rooted in the United Methodist tradition. We gather for worship, fellowship, and service to our community.

We believe the church is a place where people can encounter God's grace, grow in faith, and learn to love God and neighbor more fully and authentically.

Our Worship

Our Sunday worship service is held at 9:30 AM. Worship includes prayer, Scripture, music, preaching, and community.

Our Mission

As a United Methodist congregation, we seek to make disciples of Jesus Christ for the transformation of the world.
